# Internal websites

The design system also supports internal websites, such as backoffice systems used by our staff.

These typically require higher information density and faster task execution.
The guidelines below describe how to apply the design in these contexts.

## Compact Mode

Internal websites frequently use [Compact Mode](/docs/docs-guidelines-modes--docs), which applies reduced sizes for [typography](/docs/brand-design-tokens-typography--docs) and [spacing](/docs/brand-design-tokens-space--docs).
This mode increases information density and improves the use of available screen space.

Compact Mode automatically adjusts all components.
As a result, they remain consistent and equally suitable for task-oriented workflows as they are for content-driven public websites.

## What sets these pages apart

Internal websites may use a [Menu](/docs/components-navigation-menu--docs) beside the page, which a public website cannot.

They also give every Grid the same `paddingVertical` of `x-large`, where a public page varies that padding along the page.
Both differences are covered where the rules are: [Page structure](/docs/pages-guidelines-page-structure--docs) and [Layout and spacing](/docs/pages-guidelines-layout-and-spacing--docs).
