# Composing your own page

A page type is an arrangement of sections these templates already use, rather than an invention.
Build a page we do not list by naming its bands and looking up what each one takes.

## The sections a page is made of

Walk your design from top to bottom and name each band as one of these:

- A **breadcrumb**, in a Grid of its own above the main landmark.
- A **content header**: the page title, an optional metadata line, and the lead paragraph.
- A **body**: the running text of the page, in a cell narrow enough to read comfortably.
- **Side-by-side blocks**: two or three short blocks that each end in a link.
- **Top tasks**: a row of Cards with a heading and a paragraph, and no image.
- **Preview cards**: Cards that do carry the image of the page they point at.
- A **Spotlight band**: a full-width coloured band, usually holding a call to action.
- A **full-bleed image**: an Image or Image Slider that runs to the edges of the page.

Naming the bands is the design decision.
Everything after it is a lookup in [Layout and spacing](/docs/pages-guidelines-layout-and-spacing--docs).

## The order to decide in

1. **Public or internal.** They differ in mode, in navigation, and in how the padding varies down the page.
2. **Content, findability, or a task.** See [Choosing a page type](/docs/pages-guidelines-choosing-a-page-type--docs) for what each group looks like.
3. **One column, or a body beside a sidebar.**
4. **Which sections, top to bottom**, in the vocabulary above.
5. **Look up** the Cell sizes and the Grid padding. Do not measure the design.

## Prefer a variant to a new page type

A page that differs from an existing template in its content rather than its structure is a variant of that template.
The [Profile Page](/docs/pages-public-profile-page--docs) covers a person, a group and three kinds of place in one template, and that is the usual shape of the answer.

Raise a genuinely new page type with the design system team before building it, so it lands here rather than in one service.
