# Product page

Lists all information about a product or service of the City.
Often includes a Call to Action Link that directs the user to a form.

## Anatomy

This schematic shows the default sections for this page type and how the grids and vertical space work.

<PageAnatomy labels={anatomyLabels} of={ProductStories} />

## Headings

- Use a level 1 Heading for the title of the page.
- Use level 2 Headings for the section titles.

## Spacing

- The [Prose](/docs/utilities-css-prose--docs) utility class sets the vertical space within the Content Header and within the body Cells, following the [vertical space](/docs/docs-designer-guide-vertical-space--docs) recommendations.
  A section heading and the list or paragraph under it each get the space that guide asks for, without a margin of their own.
- The [Call to Action Link](/docs/components-navigation-call-to-action-link--docs) takes a Grid Cell of its own, between the two body Cells.
  The row gap of `x-large` then sets it apart from the text on either side, which is what makes it stand out.
