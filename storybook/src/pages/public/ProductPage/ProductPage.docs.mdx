## Usage guidelines

### When to use

Use this page for a product or service a resident can arrange, where the page has to end in a next step.

### When not to use

Use an [Information Page](/docs/pages-public-information-page--docs) for a subject a reader wants to understand rather than arrange.

## Structure

The body ends in a [Call to Action Link](/docs/components-navigation-call-to-action-link--docs) to the next step, such as an application.

## Layout and spacing

- The [Prose](/docs/utilities-css-prose--docs) utility class sets the vertical space within the Content Header and within the body Cell.
  A section heading, the list or paragraph under it, and the Call to Action Link each get the space the [vertical space](/docs/docs-designer-guide-vertical-space--docs) guide asks for, without a margin of their own.
