# News overview page

This page gives an overview of the latest news from the city.
It helps residents quickly scan recent items and dive into the ones that interest them.

The most recent articles are highlighted at the top in vertical Cards on a Grid.
Each Card combines an image, a tagline, a heading that links to the article, and a short summary.

Older articles follow under the ‘Meer nieuws’ heading as a stacked list of horizontal Cards.
Because the Cards sit in wide Grid cells, they automatically switch to a horizontal layout, with the image beside the content.
On narrow screens the same Cards fall back to their vertical layout.
