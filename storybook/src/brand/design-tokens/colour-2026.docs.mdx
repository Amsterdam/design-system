# Colour (2026)

This page shows the new colour palette as a wheel.
It has neutrals and four groups of colours, each with main colours, tints and shades.
The design tokens do not use it yet, see [Colour](/docs/brand-design-tokens-colour--docs) for the colours in use today.

## Palette

The centre of the wheel holds the neutrals.
Around it, each quarter is a group of colours: red, blue, green and yellow.
Moving outwards, a group has the lightest tint, two light tints, four main colours, two dark shades and the darkest shade.
Tints are lighter than the main colours and shades are darker.
